# Submission terms (version 1)

> **Draft for legal review.** These terms were written in plain language for the fmIDE community library and have not yet been reviewed by a lawyer. They will be reviewed with fmIDE's other licence texts before the library accepts submissions from other people. Wording may change; the version number will change with it.

By opening a pull request that adds a library pack to this repository, and ticking the box in its template, you (the **submitter**, the GitHub account that opens the pull request) agree to the following.

## 1. It is yours to share

Every template, recipe and function in the pack is your own work, or you have the right to share it on these terms. The one exception is an item you share again exactly as another author's approved pack holds it, with its record of where it came from.

## 2. The licence

You license every item in the pack that is your own work to everyone under the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0)**. Anyone may use, copy, change and share the items, commercially too, as long as they credit you. Items you share again stay under their original author's CC BY 4.0 licence.

A CC BY 4.0 licence can't be taken back for copies people already have. If your pack is later taken down, copies already downloaded remain usable under CC BY 4.0.

## 3. Your name and account

- The **author name** in your pack is published with every item and shown in fmIDE as the credit. Use the same author name every time. It is recorded against your GitHub account in `authors.json`.
- Your **GitHub account** (its login and numeric id) is recorded in `authors.json`, `packs.json` and `families.json`, and shown publicly when the library is public.
- Don't put personal information you don't want published (other than your author name) in a pack's title, description, tags, notes or items.

## 4. What the library does with a pack

- **Review:** the maintainer reviews every pack and may accept or refuse it, without having to give a reason.
- **No edits:** once approved, a pack is never changed. A new version of your work goes in a new pack.
- **Families:** the first approved pack holding a template or function family makes your account its owner. Only the owner adds versions to it. The maintainer may change an owner only in a pull request of its own.
- **Takedowns:** the maintainer may remove a pack at any time, for example after a report that it holds something that wasn't the submitter's to share. Its records stay, so its ids are never used again.
- **Publishing:** approved packs may be published on the fmIDE website's library catalogue, with their title, author, description, tags and items.

## 5. No warranty

Items are shared **as is**. Nobody (neither you, the other authors nor the maintainer) promises that an item is correct or suitable for any purpose. Anyone using an item checks it themselves before relying on it.

## 6. Changes to these terms

A new version of these terms applies to packs submitted after it is published. Packs already approved stay under the version they were submitted with.
